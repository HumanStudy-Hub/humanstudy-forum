## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original editorial brand illustration for HumanStudy Hub, a research community about human behavior and social science; portrait composition for the right margin of a reading website. Create a sophisticated two-ink editorial etching / contemporary scientific field-notebook collage, distinct original composition. Subject: three delicately drawn real human figures in different small observational scenes: two people talking across a small table, a person pausing at two branching paths, and a researcher making notes on an open sheet. These scenes are connected sparingly by pencil observation lines, a few open annotation brackets and tiny plotted dots, suggesting choices, experimental conditions and evidence. Human figures and gestures are the focal point. Elegant hand-drawn copperplate/graphite fine linework with restrained teal ink washes and a very small terracotta accent; charcoal #23444a, deep teal #155e75, pale cyan #c7e4e4. Lots of genuinely transparent negative space, irregular open edges, no rectangular background. Composition: vertical, objects concentrated in upper two-thirds, airy arrangement, complete figures within canvas so no accidental edge clipping. Mood: thoughtful, warm, observant, scholarly, grown-up magazine editorial; high craft, confident lines, subtle paper-grain ink texture only within drawn objects. No words, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Avoid maps, globes, topographic watercolor, circular celestial planets, brains, neurons, AI robots, circuit boards, corporate flat vector people, UI cards, stock-science icons, clipart, gradients, 3D. This must be a new HumanStudy identity, not an imitation of another forum's artwork.
